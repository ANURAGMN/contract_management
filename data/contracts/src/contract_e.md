# CUSTOMER SUPPORT SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Customer Support Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 28 January 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Acme Retail India Private Limited ("Buyer") and TalkBridge BPO Services Private Limited ("Supplier"). Supplier reference: TALKBRIDGE.

## 1. Term and Renewal
The Initial Term runs from the Effective Date until 27 January 2027. This Agreement renews for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives ninety (90) days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term.

## 2. Contract Value
The monthly fee is INR 5,00,000 (Indian Rupees five lakh), for an annual value of INR 60,00,000.

## 3. Payment Terms
The Buyer shall pay undisputed invoices within forty-five (45) days (Net 45).

## 4. Service Levels
The Supplier shall meet a measurable service level of 95% of support tickets answered on time per calendar month. For each percentage point below the 95% target, the Buyer is entitled to a service credit of 5% of the monthly fee, provided that total service credits in any month shall not exceed 20% of the monthly fee.

## 5. Pricing
The Charges shall increase by eight percent (8%) on each anniversary of the Effective Date. The increase applies automatically and does not require the Buyer's consent.

## 6. Limitation of Liability
The aggregate liability of each party shall not exceed the total fees paid by the Buyer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

## 7. Indemnity
The Supplier shall indemnify the Buyer for third-party claims arising from the Services, subject to the cap in Clause 6, except that indemnities for data breach shall remain uncapped.

## 8. Data Protection
The Services involve processing of personal data of the Buyer's customers. The parties have executed a Data Processing Agreement, attached as Schedule 1, which governs all such processing.

## 9. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Bengaluru shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 10. Assignment
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, save that either party may assign to an affiliate on notice.

## 11. Termination for Convenience
The Buyer may terminate for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice.
