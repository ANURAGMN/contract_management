# SAAS SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This SaaS Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 1 April 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Acme Retail India Private Limited ("Buyer") and PeopleFirst HR Software Private Limited ("Supplier").

## 1. Service
The Supplier provides a cloud-based human-resources management platform. The platform stores and processes personal data of the Buyer's employees, including names, contact details, compensation and performance records.

## 2. Term
The Initial Term is twenty-four (24) months from the Effective Date. Either party may give sixty (60) days' notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. Contract Value
The annual subscription fee is INR 24,00,000 (Indian Rupees twenty-four lakh).

## 4. Payment Terms
The Buyer shall pay each invoice within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date (Net 15).

## 5. Service Availability
The Supplier will use commercially reasonable efforts to keep the platform available. The Supplier does not commit to any specific uptime percentage, and no service credits are payable for unavailability.

## 6. Limitation of Liability
The aggregate liability of each party under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by the Buyer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

## 7. Indemnity
The Supplier shall indemnify the Buyer against third-party intellectual-property claims, subject to the limitation of liability in Clause 6.

## 8. Pricing
Fees are fixed for the Initial Term. On renewal, any increase shall not exceed the lower of CPI or five percent (5%).

## 9. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Bengaluru shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 10. Assignment
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

## 11. Termination for Convenience
The Buyer may terminate for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice.

## 12. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep confidential the other party's confidential information and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement.
