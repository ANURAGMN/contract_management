# FACILITIES MANAGEMENT STATEMENT OF WORK

This Statement of Work ("SOW") is entered into on 1 March 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Acme Retail India Private Limited ("Buyer") and CleanSpace Global Facilities Pte. Ltd. ("Supplier").

## 1. Term and Renewal
The Initial Term is twelve (12) months from the Effective Date. Thereafter this SOW shall automatically renew for successive terms of twenty-four (24) months each, unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 2. Contract Value
The annual charge is INR 36,00,000 (Indian Rupees thirty-six lakh).

## 3. Payment Terms
The Buyer shall pay undisputed invoices within forty-five (45) days (Net 45).

===PAGE===

## 4. Service Levels
The Supplier shall meet a measurable service level of 98% scheduled-task completion per month. Service credits of 2% of the monthly fee apply per percentage point missed, capped at 10% of the monthly fee.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
The aggregate liability of each party shall not exceed the fees paid in the prior twelve (12) months. This limitation is the sole and exhaustive cap on liability under this SOW.

## 6. Indemnity
The Supplier shall indemnify the Buyer for third-party claims arising from the Services, subject to the limitation of liability in Clause 5, except that indemnities for data breach shall remain uncapped.

## 7. Pricing
Charges are fixed for each term. Any change on renewal shall not exceed five percent (5%) per annum.

===PAGE===

## 8. Assignment
The Supplier may freely assign, novate or subcontract this SOW, in whole or in part, to any third party without the prior consent of the Buyer.

## 9. Data Protection
The Services do not involve the processing of personal data of the Buyer's customers or employees. No personal data is shared under this SOW.

## 10. Termination for Convenience
The Buyer may terminate this SOW for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice.

## 11. Governing Law
This SOW is governed by the laws of Singapore, and the courts of Singapore shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute.

## 12. Notices
All notices under this SOW shall be sent to: for the Buyer, the Head of Procurement, Acme Retail India Private Limited, Bengaluru; for the Supplier, the Contracts Manager, CleanSpace Global Facilities Pte. Ltd., Singapore. A notice is deemed received two business days after dispatch.
