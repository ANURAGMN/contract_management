# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of 1 January 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Acme Retail India Private Limited, a company incorporated under the laws of India ("Buyer"), and Brightline IT Services LLP ("Supplier").

## 1. Term
This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until 31 December 2027 (the "Initial Term"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms. This Agreement does not renew automatically. Either party may propose renewal in writing no later than 60 days before the end of the then-current term.

## 2. Charges and Contract Value
The total contract value over the Initial Term is INR 1,20,00,000 (Indian Rupees one crore twenty lakh). Fees are fixed for the Initial Term and are not subject to increase during that period.

## 3. Payment Terms
The Buyer shall pay all undisputed invoices within forty-five (45) days of the date of a valid invoice (Net 45).

===PAGE===

## 4. Service Levels
The Supplier shall maintain a measurable service level of 99.0% platform availability per calendar month. Where availability falls below the target, the Buyer is entitled to service credits of 2% of the monthly fee for each percentage point missed, capped at 15% of the monthly fee for that month.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
The total aggregate liability of each party under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by the Buyer to the Supplier in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes liability for fraud, fraudulent misrepresentation, or death or personal injury caused by negligence.

## 6. Indemnity
The Supplier shall indemnify the Buyer against third-party claims arising from the Services. The Supplier's indemnification obligations are subject to the limitation of liability in Clause 5, except that indemnities for intellectual property infringement and for data breach shall remain uncapped.

## 7. Data Protection
The parties have entered into a Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") in the form attached as Schedule 2, which governs the processing of any personal data under this Agreement and is incorporated by reference.

## 8. Termination for Convenience
The Buyer may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time on thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the Supplier.

## 9. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Any dispute shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration at a mutually agreed neutral venue under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

## 10. Assignment
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, save that either party may assign to an affiliate on written notice.

## 11. Pricing Stability
Fees are fixed for the term of this Agreement. No price increase shall apply during the Initial Term.
