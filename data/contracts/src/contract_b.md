# LOGISTICS MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Logistics Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 1 February 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Acme Retail India Private Limited ("Buyer") and Vector Logistics Private Limited ("Supplier").

## 1. Term
The Initial Term begins on the Effective Date and ends on 31 January 2027. Renewal shall be by mutual written agreement; this Agreement does not renew automatically.

## 2. Contract Value
The estimated annual contract value is INR 80,00,000 (Indian Rupees eighty lakh).

## 3. Payment Terms
The Buyer shall pay undisputed invoices within forty-five (45) days of invoice date (Net 45).

## 4. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Bengaluru shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

===PAGE===

## 5. Limitation of Liability
Save as set out in this Clause 5, the total aggregate liability of each party under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. The foregoing limitation shall not apply to the Supplier's obligations except as set out in Clause 12 (Indemnity), which shall be governed by the terms of that Clause.

## 6. Service Levels
The Supplier shall use reasonable endeavours to deliver consignments on time. The parties shall review performance quarterly. Service credits of 3% of the monthly fee apply for each percentage point below a 96% on-time target, capped at 20% of the monthly fee.

## 7. Pricing
The Supplier may increase the Charges at its sole discretion upon thirty (30) days' written notice to the Buyer. The Buyer's continued use of the Services after the notice period constitutes acceptance of the revised Charges.

===PAGE===

## 8. Data Protection
The parties shall enter into a Data Processing Agreement consistent with applicable data protection law before any processing of personal data commences.

## 9. Assignment
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

## 10. Termination
This Agreement may be terminated by either party for material breach that remains uncured for thirty (30) days. Neither party may terminate this Agreement for convenience.

## 11. Intellectual Property Indemnity
The Supplier shall indemnify the Buyer against all claims that the Services infringe any third-party intellectual property right. The Supplier's liability under this Clause 11 for intellectual property infringement shall be uncapped.

===PAGE===

## 12. General Indemnity
The Supplier shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Buyer from and against any and all claims, losses, liabilities, damages, costs and expenses of any kind arising out of or in connection with the Services. The Supplier's obligations under this Clause 12 shall not be subject to any limitation of liability, and the cap set out in Clause 5 shall not apply to this Clause 12.

## 13. Notices
All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the registered addresses of the parties set out above.
