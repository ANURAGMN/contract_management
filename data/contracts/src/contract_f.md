# PAYMENTS PLATFORM SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Payments Platform Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 1 March 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Acme Retail India Private Limited ("Buyer") and Ledgerline Payments Technology Private Limited ("Supplier"). Supplier reference: LEDGERLINE.

## 1. Term and Renewal
The Initial Term is twelve (12) months from the Effective Date. This Agreement does not renew automatically; any renewal shall be agreed in writing.

## 2. Contract Value
The annual subscription fee is INR 72,00,000 (Indian Rupees seventy-two lakh), invoiced quarterly in advance.

## 3. Payment Terms
The Buyer shall pay undisputed invoices within forty-five (45) days of receipt (Net 45).

## 4. Service Levels
The Supplier shall maintain a measurable service level of 99.9% platform availability per calendar month. Where availability falls below the target, the Buyer is entitled to service credits of 5% of the monthly fee for each percentage point missed, capped at 20% of the monthly fee.

## 5. Data
In providing the Services the Supplier will collect, store and process personal data of the Buyer's customers, including names, mobile numbers and payment card details. The Supplier will apply its standard security practices.

## 6. Pricing
Fees are fixed for the Initial Term.

## 7. Limitation of Liability
There is no ceiling on the amount either party may recover from the other under or in connection with this Agreement.

## 8. Indemnity
The Supplier shall indemnify the Buyer against all losses arising from the Services. The indemnity in this Clause 8 operates independently of, and is not limited by, Clause 7 or any other limitation in this Agreement.

## 9. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 10. Assignment
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

## 11. Termination for Convenience
The Buyer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice.
