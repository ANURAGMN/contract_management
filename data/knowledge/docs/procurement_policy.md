# Procurement Policy (Demo)

> Synthetic demo data — not real company policy or legal advice.

## Budget and approval thresholds

Annual software spend up to ₹48 lakh may be approved by the Procurement Owner.
Spend above ₹48 lakh, or any discount/concession outside the approved negotiation range, requires Finance co-approval.

## Preferred contract term

Preferred initial term is 12 months. Multi-year terms require Business Owner justification and Procurement approval.

## Exception policy

Policy exceptions (price, term, SLA, liability) must be recorded as approval tasks with reason codes.
No agent or automated system may self-approve an exception.
