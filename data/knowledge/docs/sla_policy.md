# SLA Policy (Demo)

> Synthetic demo data — not real company policy or legal advice.

## Minimum uptime

The minimum acceptable platform uptime is 99.9% measured per calendar month.
Offers below 99.9% are an operational exception and must be routed to the Business Owner and Procurement.

## Service credits

Preferred service credits are at least 5% of monthly fees per percentage point missed, capped at 20% of monthly fees.
Measurement window is calendar month unless otherwise approved.

## Support response

P1 incidents: response within 1 hour. P2: within 4 hours during business hours.
