# Legal Clause Playbook (Demo)

> Synthetic demo data — not real company policy or legal advice.

## Liability

Preferred: aggregate liability capped at fees paid in the prior 12 months (trailing-12).
Fallback: 1x annual fees. Walk-away: uncapped general liability.

## Indemnity

Preferred: indemnity within the liability cap except IP and data-breach carve-outs.
Walk-away: uncapped general indemnity.

## Auto-renewal approved wording

Preferred clause: This Agreement shall renew for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives at least sixty (60) days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term.

## SLA approved wording

Preferred clause: The Supplier shall meet a measurable service level of at least 99.9% platform availability per calendar month, with service credits of 5% of the monthly fee per percentage point missed, capped at 20% of the monthly fee.

## Data protection

Preferred: parties enter a DPA consistent with applicable data protection law before personal data processing begins.

## Termination for convenience

Preferred: Buyer may terminate for convenience on 30 days' notice. Fallback: 60 days' notice.
