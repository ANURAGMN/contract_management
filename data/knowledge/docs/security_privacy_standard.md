> Synthetic demo data — not real company policy or legal advice.

## Data processing agreement

Where personal data is processed, a signed Data Processing Agreement (DPA) or equivalent clause is mandatory before go-live.
Missing DPA is a Security/Privacy and Legal exception.

## Data residency and subprocessors

Supplier must disclose subprocessors and data residency. Changes require written notice and Security review.

## Incident notification

Security incidents affecting buyer data must be notified within 72 hours of discovery.
