# Renewal & Exit Policy (Demo)

> Synthetic demo data — not real company policy or legal advice.

## Auto-renewal notice period

Preferred auto-renewal notice period is 60 days before the end of the then-current term.
Notice of 90 days is longer than preferred and is a procurement process burden (deviation), not automatically a legal violation.
Notice shorter than 30 days is a walk-away for Legal/Procurement review.

## Renewal ownership

Procurement owns renewal reminders. Legal reviews auto-renew language when notice is below 30 days or renewal term exceeds 12 months.

## Exit and data return

On termination or non-renewal, supplier must return or securely delete buyer data within 30 days and provide reasonable exit assistance.
